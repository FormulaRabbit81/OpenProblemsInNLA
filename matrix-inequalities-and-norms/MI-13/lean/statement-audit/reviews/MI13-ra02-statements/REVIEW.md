With `J(Y)=[X*,Y*]`, direct multiplication gives `J(Y)=-(DY)*`, `J^2=-T`, and `TJ=JT`. This last identity is valid although J is conjugate-linear, since the scalar minus one is real. Cyclic trace gives `<Y,JY>=0` with the exact signs shown.
