# Exact proposed MI-13 obligations, before statement/code implementation

These are prose signatures for mathematical review, not frozen Lean declarations. Numerical statements must be finalized before proof bodies. No assumption may encode the refined commutator conclusion or a valid SVD's existence.

1. **Canonical full target.** For every naturals m,n with 2<=m and 2<=n, all `A,C : Matrix (Fin m) (Fin n) Complex` and all `B : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin m) Complex`, prove `F(ABC-CBA)^2 <= 2 * O(B)^2 * (sv(A,0)^2 + sv(A,1)^2) * F(C)^2`. Here F is the genuine Frobenius norm, O the genuine Euclidean operator norm, and sv is the actual zero-indexed, decreasing singular-value sequence of A's map between Euclidean spaces. Invertibility, nonzero values, real entries, square shapes and distinct singular values are not global assumptions.
2. **Semantic identities.** F(X)^2 is the finite sum of squared complex entry norms and the real trace of X*X. O(X) equals sv(X,0), including X=0. sv is nonnegative, antitone, and its squares are the decreasing eigenvalues of X*X, extended by zero. Flattening matrices to Euclidean space preserves the Hilbert–Schmidt product. Explicit matrix norm instances must prevent accidental use of the entrywise sup norm.
3. **Full SVD existence.** Every square complex X, including zero and rank-deficient X, has unitary U,V and a nonnegative decreasing diagonal whose entries equal the actual sv(X,i), with X=U diag(sv) V*. Construct right vectors from ordered Gram eigenvectors; normalized images for positive values are orthonormal and must be extended on zero values using the available orthonormal-basis extension. Establish the necessary rank/dimension statements. Rectangular padding gets an explicit singular-value preservation theorem, not an asserted convention.
4. **Finite commutator spectral obligations.** With D(Y)=XY-YX and J(Y)=X*Y*-Y*X*, prove D's Hilbert-space adjoint is ad(X*), T=D*D is self-adjoint positive, J is conjugate-linear, J^2=-T, TJ=JT, `<Y,JY>=0`, and `F(JY)=F(DY)`. For each positive real eigenvalue lambda of T and nonzero eigenvector Y, prove Y,JY are independent eigenvectors. A complex-linear functional on their span has a nonzero kernel vector. Separately prove the largest-eigenvalue variational bound; handle largest eigenvalue zero without division.
5. **Exact scalar inequality.** For all real s,t>=0 and all complex p,q, `|s*p-t*q|^2 <= (s^2+t^2)(|p|^2+|q|^2)`. This is symbolic Cauchy–Schwarz, not finitely sampled data. For descending nonnegative finite s and every (i,j) except (0,0), prove `s_i^2+s_j^2 <= s_0^2+s_1^2`; r>=2 supplies the second entry. The resulting refined commutator bound must be proved, not supplied as a hypothesis.
6. **Contraction averaging and the only scalar certificate.** For every real d in [0,1], prove `d^2 + sqrt(1-d^2)^2 = 1` and hence unit modulus of `d +/- i sqrt(1-d^2)`. The exact certificate `0 < (1/2 : Real)` can be generated with pinned LeanCert in default-kernel mode and consumed when simplifying the norm of the two half-scaled summands. Prove `(1/2)+(1/2)=1` exactly. No finite interval grid is required, and LeanCert must not be used as a surrogate for the universal algebraic argument.
7. **Reduction and boundary obligations.** Unitary invariance of F and sv; `(AWC-CWA)W=[AW,CW]`; average decomposition of every square contraction; scaling by the positive actual O(B) only after B!=0; B=0 separately. For r=max(m,n), prove actual E/F matrix identities, padded product equality, Frobenius/operator norms and first-two singular values preserved. Explicitly retain r>=2 and rank-zero/rank-one cases.
8. **Exact endpoint check.** With A=diag(1,-1), B=I2, C=e12, show F(commutator)^2=4 and O(B)^2=1, sv(A,0)^2=sv(A,1)^2=F(C)^2=1. This validates normalization and sharpness, not universal truth.

MI-16's full general algebraic prescription and MI-27's full coefficient-one inequality remain deferred with their original quantifiers; no partial theorem about them will be labeled a complete formalization. All future target drafts require two independent reviews, real elaboration and an immutable freeze before authoring proofs, followed by actual default-kernel and Linux Comparator acceptance before a completed count.
