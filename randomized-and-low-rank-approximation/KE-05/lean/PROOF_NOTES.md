# KE-05 proof implementation

George Stepaniants's full argument is implemented without replacing the original recurrence, Gaussian model or norms. Nian Shao's interpolation framework and conjecture retain their original credit. Sidney Holden's formalization was developed with AI assistance.

`Recurrence.lean` proves the exact descending fold contract, including every prescribed prefix and final update, first for arbitrary initialization. `RecurrenceUnique.lean` proves uniqueness by descending indices: a prefix uses only higher-index hats. Thus the literal zero-initialized definition equals a convenient initialized fold with no extra assumption. `IdentityWitness.lean` proves that every root ordering is nonsingular at the identity sample for every admissible diagonal family, using distinct-block spectral gaps and genuine diagonal products.

`PolynomialNull.lean` proves that a nonzero multivariate polynomial vanishes with probability zero for finite independent atomless coordinate measures. Its finite-dimensional induction uses a nonzero coefficient, finite univariate root sets, Fubini, and an actual measure-preserving coordinate equivalence. The Gaussian corollary uses the product of the specified standard normal laws. `Regular.lean` encodes expressions regular at an explicit witness and constructs polynomial numerator/denominator representations. Inversion retains the prior denominator guard (q²/(pq)); cancellation never silently extends an identity over a singular locus. `RegularMatrix.lean` covers actual determinant, adjugate, inverse and matrix operations. `RegularRecurrence.lean` propagates these representations and the identity-witness invariants through every step. `GenericValidity.lean` then proves almost-sure validity simultaneously for every root ordering, and entry measurability. `Measurability.lean` and `TotalMeasurability.lean` cover the actual norm, real finite suprema, endpoint zero/zero convention and invalid-sample branch on the whole sample space.

`Numerics.lean` proves the specified rational sample exactly. Its limit norm 68/7 is derived through an actual Euclidean rank-one operator and vector norm identities. `Endpoints.lean` proves admissibility, both genuine real infimum/supremum endpoints, and the actual cross-gap value. These are exact algebraic certificates, not probabilistic evidence about an event containing that one point.

`FirstOrdering.lean` unfolds the actual k=0, d=3 recurrence. It proves S23=eX-P and S13=(2I-hat1)(2I-P), with det(S13)=2(2-e)(2-2e). `LimitAlgebra.lean` derives det X=2, det P=0, trace P=1 and adj(P)=I-P from genuine similarities. It proves det(eX-P)=e(2e-kappa), then cancels e only after establishing all nonzero denominators from validity. The actual hat1 becomes

    (2e-kappa)^(-1) • ((e adj(X)-(I-P)) X (eX-P)).

`GenericWitness.lean` uses the rational sample solely to show that the kappa and Q00 polynomial numerators are nonzero. `MatrixLimit.lean` combines their Gaussian almost-sure nonvanishing, countably many valid epsilon instances, and the exact canceled formula. This yields the claimed matrix limit -Q/kappa and proves that its actual operator norm is strictly positive.

`MatrixBounds.lean` derives |det A|≤norm(A)^2 from Euclidean column norms, yielding the inverse half-norm lower bound from 0<det S≤8. `ExtremumBounds.lean` embeds the selected root/endpoint quantities into the genuine bounded finite suprema. `Asymptotic.lean` discharges the determinant assumptions for every e in (0,1/4), proves the literal full lower bound, and uses the nonzero limit and epsilon=1/(m+6) to force the actual total constant to positive infinity almost surely. `ProbabilityBridge.lean` applies finite-measure indicator convergence to obtain the marginal probability limit, a deterministic admissible counterexample for each C, and the full universally quantified conjecture's negation.
