\section*{Complete original target}
For a complex Hermitian positive-semidefinite matrix
$A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$, set $R_0=A$. At a nonzero residual, select
$j$ with conditional probability $(R_t)_{jj}/\operatorname{tr}(R_t)$ and set
\[
 R_{t+1}=R_t-\frac{R_t(:,j)R_t(j,:)}{(R_t)_{jj}}.
\]
Zero residuals remain zero; a zero diagonal has zero probability at a
nonzero residual. Let $\lambda_1(A)\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_n(A)\ge0$ be the
actual ordered eigenvalues and $\tau_r(A)=\sum_{j>r}\lambda_j(A)$.

The theorem \texttt{NLA.RA02.no\_polynomial\_trace\_factor} proves that
there are no real constants $C>0$ and $p\ge0$, independent of $n,r,A$,
such that
\[
 \mathbb E\operatorname{tr}(R_r)\le C r^p\tau_r(A)
\]
for every $n\ge1$, every complex Hermitian positive-semidefinite $A$, and
every integer $1\le r\le n$, after exactly $r$ pivots.
The stronger constructive export
\texttt{NLA.RA02.universal\_counterexamples} supplies, for every such
real $C,p$, a rank parameter $r\ge1$ and an actual positive-definite witness
$A\in\mathbb C^{(r+1)\times(r+1)}$ with
\[
 0<\tau_r(A),\qquad
 C r^p\tau_r(A)<\mathbb E\operatorname{tr}(R_r).
\]

The formal process includes every ordered history, including repeated and
zero-probability choices. Its finite path weights are proved nonnegative
and normalized, with the actual conditional recursion. Uniform dummy labels
after absorption do not change the zero residual. The tail uses Mathlib's
genuinely decreasing Hermitian eigenvalues, with a proved Euclidean Rayleigh
bound; it is not defined by the counterexample's desired estimate.

\section*{Finite proof and computation}
For $r\ge1$, the proof uses an explicit real-entry arrowhead matrix of order
$r+1$. With
\[
 t=\frac1{2r+1},\quad \varepsilon=t^{2(r+1)},\quad
 d_i=\varepsilon^i,\quad \alpha_i=t^{i+1}\quad(0\le i<r),
\]
and $D=\operatorname{diag}(d_0,\ldots,d_{r-1})$, it is
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}
 D&D\alpha\\
 \alpha^{T}D&\varepsilon^r+\alpha^{T}D\alpha
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its exact complex quadratic form proves positive definiteness. A nonzero
Rayleigh probe gives $0<\tau_r(A)\le\varepsilon^r$. Exact residual-state
identities, positive prefix traces and an injective binary history encoding
give
\[
 \mathbb E\operatorname{tr}(R_r)
 \ge \frac{2^r\varepsilon^r}{(1+1/r)^r}
 \ge \frac{2^r}{3}\,\tau_r(A).
\]
The retained $2^r$ histories lower-bound the full normalized expectation;
they are not renormalized into a different sampling law. Exponential
domination of every real power then yields the strict violations above.

The only numerical certificate is $\exp(1)\le3$, proved by LeanCert with
explicit kernel trust. It is consumed by
\texttt{rank\_denominator\_le\_three}, then
\texttt{exponential\_tail\_factor} and the final counterexample chain.
All rank, history, matrix and real-exponent calculations remain symbolic;
there is no interval subdivision or numerical eigenvalue approximation.

The sufficient factor $2^r/3$ resolves the complete original question.
The manuscript's sharper limiting ratio $2^r$, entrywise-positive and
correlation-matrix extensions, its separate LU results, and oversampling
are not additional formal claims of this project.
