\section*{Original target and exact formal theorem}
For a real square matrix $Y$, its comparison matrix has entries
\[
 \mathcal M(Y)_{ii}=|Y_{ii}|,\qquad
 \mathcal M(Y)_{ij}=-|Y_{ij}|\quad(i\ne j).
\]
It is a nonsingular H-matrix if $\mathcal M(Y)=sI-B$ for an entrywise
nonnegative real matrix $B$ with $s>\rho(B)$, where $\rho$ is the full complex
algebraic spectral radius.
For every $n\ge1$ and every nonsingular real H-matrix
$A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ with strictly positive diagonal, define
\[
 X_0=A,\qquad X_{k+1}=\tfrac12(X_k+X_k^{-1}A)\quad(k\ge0).
\]
The Lean theorem \texttt{NLA.SF01.canonical\_newton\_preservation} proves
that every $X_k$ is invertible and a real nonsingular H-matrix with strictly
positive diagonal. Every positive dimension and every iteration is included.
No symmetry, normality, entrywise positivity, finite iteration bound or
assumed rational representation is imposed.

The proof establishes both directions between the original spectral condition
and positive diagonal-dominance weights. Actual shifted resolvent domination
and block inverse identities produce a finite rational invariant for every
Newton step. Empty pole families, repeated poles and zero weights are included.
The manuscript's scaled or affine initializations and Halley extension are
outside this formal claim; they are not needed for the original target.
