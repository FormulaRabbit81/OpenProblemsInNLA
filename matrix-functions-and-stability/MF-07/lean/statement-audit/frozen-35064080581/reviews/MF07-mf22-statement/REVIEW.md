## Mathematical fidelity and attempts to break it

The final theorem has the required order `∀ d≥1, ∃ Θ>0, ∀ M, ∀ n≥1, ∀ A`. The constant is selected before the family, length and word. `M : Set (Matrix (Fin d) (Fin d) ℂ)` may be infinite; its only structural premises are compactness and nonemptiness. The radius-one premise is the original one once the separately required correspondence theorem is proved. No finite-generation, irreducibility, normality, diagonalizability, real-field restriction, family-dependent constant or supplied extremal norm appears in the final theorem.

`spectralNorm` is the operator norm of the actual continuous linear map on complex Euclidean space. The pinned Mathlib `toEuclideanCLM` definition uses the Euclidean orthonormal basis and its application is ordinary matrix-vector multiplication. It does not use the default entrywise matrix norm. The matrix compactness topology is the ordinary finite-dimensional coordinate topology; choosing the operator norm explicitly does not change its compact sets. `matrixProduct w = w.reverse.prod` and `List.ofFn` retain chronological order, multiplicity and arbitrary choices. The nil/append contract gives identity and left multiplication by the later generator.

The supremum definitions contain actual generator and word norms. Attainment and every-generator/every-word domination are independent obligations, rather than assumed in a custom structure. For a positive dimension, a compact nonempty family gives a compact nonempty finite product for each length, so these maximum statements include infinitely generated families. At length zero there is the empty word and the identity has operator norm one. The bound by `L^0=1` remains correct when `L=0`. A zero family has positive-length growth zero and is not accidentally a radius-one example.

The JSR is the infimum of actual positive-length roots, not a constant, an existential seed or a predicate hiding the desired growth bound. The draft requires submultiplicativity and the exact equivalence between this value being one and the original root sequence tending to one. This is sufficient for the complete MF-07 domain and its contrapositive; neither implication is omitted. The definition's arbitrary zero-index root value is excluded from the infimum and cannot affect an at-top limit. Nonnegative bounded suprema, positive-length nonemptiness and any zero-growth case still need real proofs. In particular, a logarithmic Fekete implementation cannot assume positivity or its bounded-below hypothesis without proving them. The draft does **not** separately export the general limit correspondence at nonunit radii. That is outside the claimed one-problem result, not a missing hypothesis in MF-07. The identity singleton is a required genuine radius-one witness, so the domain cannot be vacuous.

`IsComplexNorm` states nonnegativity, separation, the triangle inequality and complex absolute homogeneity. The equivalent approximate norm and the rounded coordinates are existential conclusions for every real `a>1`. Their constants and coordinates may depend on the family and `a`, as the original proof allows, but disappear from the final product estimate. The unitary identities are both genuine matrix equations; the sorted diagonal entries are strictly positive; the entry bound uses the original maximum `L`. No norm existence or product estimate has been smuggled into a final premise.

The damping predicate has the correct orientation: with nonincreasing weights, it kills an entry only when its row weight strictly exceeds its column weight. Consequently every full equal-weight diagonal block is retained, while the strictly upper inter-block part vanishes. Scalar weights leave every matrix permitted and produce equality under conjugation. Positive weights make the inverse diagonal genuine; zero weights are excluded exactly where inversion is used. The separate diagonal-inverse obligation covers those equations. ## Numerical boundary and constant

The chosen constant is `Θ₁=1` and `Θ_d=d(6d²/(d−1))^(d−1)` for `d≥2`. This is a valid enlargement of the manuscript's existentially admissible constant, provided the explicitly required `exp(1)≤3` certificate is proved and consumed. The source question does not prescribe the sharper constant. The threshold statement quantifies every `d≥2`, `n≥1` and `L≥1`; its denominator is therefore positive.
