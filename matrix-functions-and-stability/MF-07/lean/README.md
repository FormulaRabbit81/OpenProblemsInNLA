# MF-07 Lean formalization

This candidate proves the complete original dimension-only polynomial product bound. For each positive dimension d, one positive constant is chosen before every nonempty compact family of complex d×d matrices with joint spectral radius one. It bounds the actual Euclidean operator norm of every positive-length switching product by Θ_d (L n)^(d−1), where L is the actual largest generator norm. Infinite compact families, reducibility, repetitions, complex entries and d=1 remain included.

The proof derives approximate extremal norms from the actual growth sequence and proves the infimum/root-limit correspondence. A maximal-determinant basis and ordered positive Gram coordinates give rounded coordinates without assuming an extremal norm. Triangular damping retains equal-weight blocks; capped weights and the exact interspersed-product expansion give a dimension-only quantitative bound. Scalar dimension one is handled separately. The formal proof uses Θ_1=1 and Θ_d=d(6d²/(d−1))^(d−1) for d≥2. The manuscript's smaller constant, sharpness, general-radius formula, real-field extension and separate MF-05 theorem are outside this formal claim.
