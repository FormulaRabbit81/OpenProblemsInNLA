# MF-07 Lean formalization

This candidate proves the complete original dimension-only polynomial product bound. For each positive dimension d, one positive constant is chosen before every nonempty compact family of complex d×d matrices with joint spectral radius one. It bounds the actual Euclidean operator norm of every positive-length switching product by Θ_d (L n)^(d−1), where L is the actual largest generator norm. Infinite compact families, reducibility, repetitions, complex entries and d=1 remain included.
