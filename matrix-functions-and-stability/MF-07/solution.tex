\textbf{Scope.} This is a formalization and verification note for the complete
original MF-07 target. The mathematical resolution remains credited to
Matthew J. Colbrook, Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics,

product bounds and sharp H\"older continuity of the joint spectral radius},

Epperlein and Wirth retain credit for the original question.

\section*{Original target and formal result}
For each positive integer $d$, there is a constant $\Theta_d>0$, chosen before
the family, such that every nonempty compact
$\mathcal M\subset\mathbb C^{d\times d}$ of joint spectral radius one satisfies
\[
 \|A_k\cdots A_1\|_2\le\Theta_d(Lk)^{d-1},\qquad
 L=\max_{A\in\mathcal M}\|A\|_2,
\]
for every $k\ge1$ and every $A_1,\ldots,A_k\in\mathcal M$.
The norm is the Euclidean operator norm and the radius is the original limit
of the maximal product norm to the power $1/k$.

The final Lean declaration is
\texttt{NLA.MF07.canonical\_uniform\_bound}.
The declaration \texttt{NLA.MF07.radius\_one\_semantics} proves the required
infimum/root-limit correspondence. Infinite compact families, reducibility,
repeated factors, arbitrary complex entries and $d=1$ are included.
The formal proof takes
\[
 \Theta_1=1,\qquad
 \Theta_d=d\left(\frac{6d^2}{d-1}\right)^{d-1}\quad(d\ge2).
\]
This proves the original existential assertion. The manuscript's smaller
constant with $2e$ in place of $6$, sharpness, general-radius formula,
bounded/real-family extensions and separate MF-05 result are outside this
formalization. No irreducibility or assumed exact extremal norm is added.
