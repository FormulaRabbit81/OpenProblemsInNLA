# Sources and dependency map

Public sources were read through the website, without a GitHub plugin. Access date: 2026-09-14.

1. Alex Townsend, OpenProblemsInNLA, AC-05 canonical statement.
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/main/arithmetic-and-complexity/AC-05/README.md
   Used for the exact tight/concise, all-dimension, grouped-power target. The page reported Open, last checked 2026-09-10.

2. OpenProblemsInNLA root README.
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/main/README.md
   Used for status definitions. No repository mutation was made.

3. Austin Conner, Fulvio Gesmundo, J. M. Landsberg and Emanuele Ventura, Rank and border rank of Kronecker powers of tensors and Strassen's laser method. Computational Complexity 31 (2022), Article 1.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.04785
   The arXiv PDF's printed page 25, Proposition 5.3, supplies first/square symmetry background. The all-power argument in this report is supplied separately. No approximate decomposition or generic border-rank table entry is used as an exact certificate.

4. Arturo Acuaviva, Visu Makam, Harold Nieuwboer, David Pérez-García, Friedrich Sittner, Michael Walter and Freek Witteveen, The minimal canonical form of a tensor network, arXiv:2209.14358v1 (2022).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14358v1
   Section 2, Theorem 2.5 and Lemma 2.6: criticality, affine closed orbits and their uniqueness. Applied after explicitly computing scalar one-factor Gram matrices.

5. Chris Woodward, Moment maps and geometric invariant theory, arXiv:0912.1132v6 (2011).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.1132v6
   Lemma 4.2.3, Proposition 4.2.5, Theorem 4.2.6 and Theorem 5.2.1: projective semistability, unique polystable orbit and the quotient. Applied to same-format general-linear degenerations after passing to projective space.

6. Alessandra Bernardi and Fulvio Gesmundo, Triangular tensor networks, pencils of matrices and beyond, arXiv:2602.15114v1 (2026).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15114v1
   Theorem 5.3: background for the triangle-network realization. The displayed 2-by-2 trace formula is verified directly here and is not claimed to improve the inherited rank bounds.

7. Josh Alman and Baitian Li, Asymptotic Rank Speedup Theorems, Revisited, arXiv:2605.21738v1 (2026).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21738v1
   Background for the inherited speedup framework. No new asymptotic numerical upper bound is asserted in this package.

8. Supplied archive AC05_rank_speedup.zip and its report, AC-05: Uniform bounds from rank-one completion, 16 pages (2026-09-14).
   Preserved byte-for-byte in baseline/. Exact checksum in README.md and results/inherited_audit.json. Its earlier included continuation is referenced only for the existing dimension-three completion criterion; the present theorems do not depend on that classification.

Bounded literature checking is not a guarantee that every related publication was located. No priority claim is made for the results in this report.
