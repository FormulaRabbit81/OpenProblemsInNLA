# Attribution and reuse notes

The Laderman and Sun factor arrays are existing mathematical algorithms. They
are included as exact data for reproduction, not claimed as original results.

- Laderman: Julian D. Laderman, Bulletin of the AMS 82 (1976), 126–128,
  DOI 10.1090/S0002-9904-1976-13988-2.
- Sun presentation: Yinqi Sun, arXiv:2604.27645v1, Section 5. The source is marked
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. The expanded forms have been transcribed
  into arrays and reverified. See https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .
  The paper attributes the underlying decomposition to earlier work and a
  cyclic reorientation; its factor presentation is the source used here.

Contraction signatures, standard changes of basis, Jacobian methods, and
parameter-family searches have prior literature cited in SOURCES.md and the
report. No priority claim is made for the specific restricted results.

prepared with ChatGPT for this request. They may be used, modified, and shared
for research and verification, with the preceding third-party attributions
preserved. No warranty of correctness or mathematical completeness is given;
