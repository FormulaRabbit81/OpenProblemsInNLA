# SP-15 Lean formalization

The original finite-unitary-class assertion is false: for every M there are M+1 complex 9-by-9 matrices with every ordered singular value equal for every complex shift, and no unitarily similar pair.

The proof checks the exact coefficient polynomials, all 90 derivative entries and an LU certificate with determinant -1088. A genuine inverse-function theorem constructs the coefficient fiber inside one radius-1/16 box. Actual Gram characteristic polynomials give all shifted singular values, with multiplicities; unrestricted unitary conjugation recovers all ten parameters. The Schur proof uses the valid sI+Q pivot, and the unitary proof uses direct block elimination. Supplementary smooth-family and Jordan-type enhancements are outside the original target and are not exported.

Four kernel-mode LeanCert rational inequalities and one exact margin identity certify the single radius-1/16 box and margin 3/4. Box bounds consume the certificate, matrix positivity consumes those bounds, and the final construction consumes positivity. All remaining arguments are symbolic; the determinant uses a nine-pivot product rather than full expansion. No interval subdivision or sampling is used.
