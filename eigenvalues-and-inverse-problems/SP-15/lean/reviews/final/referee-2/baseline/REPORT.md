The formalization chooses the valid submersion point with `c=d=1`; it need not reuse the paper's illustrative `d=0` point. The 1/16 box is an open nonempty construction domain, not a restriction on the matrices quantified in the original problem. Strict row bounds give the positive 3/4 diagonal margin throughout the box. Gershgorin and Hermitian eigenvalue positivity establish Q positivity; P is a positive diagonal matrix. CFC nonnegativity gives Hermitian roots, the square identities follow from positive-semidefinite input, and input positive definiteness gives root units by the unit-square lemma. No root identity or invertibility is assumed in Definitions.

3. **Actual coefficient and derivative bridge.** `coefficient_determinant_identity` expands the actual three-by-three matrix, handles the complex conjugate pair, and proves the entire nine-coefficient polynomial identity for arbitrary real parameters and arbitrary complex `u,s`. The nine derivative row files apply actual strict product, sum and power rules to those same polynomials. Equality of the resulting linear maps is checked on an arbitrary perturbation vector, with all ten input slots present. These are not assertions that a numerical matrix is the derivative by definition. The LU row files establish all 81 entries of the actual first-nine-column minor, followed by triangular identities and a nine-pivot determinant product of -1088; no factorial-sized determinant expansion is used.

4. **Inverse function theorem and actual fiber.** The augmented map retains the original tenth coordinate `d`. Its first nine derivative rows come from the proved strict derivative and its last row is the genuine projection. Expanding only the single nonzero last-row term ties its determinant to the certified minor. The finite-dimensional continuous-linear-equivalence API supplies the inverse derivative, and Mathlib's strict inverse-function theorem supplies an actual local inverse. Its neighborhood is restricted into the already proved open box. Pullback along the constant-coefficient/free-d line yields a positive interval with coefficient equality and `ψ(t) 9 = t`. 5. **Every complex shift and every singular value.** `BlockGram` derives the actual conjugate-transpose product and transports it by the fixed reindexing.
